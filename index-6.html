Answer a few questions about the behaviours and symptoms you're seeing. NeuroMap will decode your child's nervous system state and build a personalised regulation plan — powered by Willow, your AI nervous system coach.

Three nervous system states

🦉
Owl
Regulated & connected. Calm, curious, able to learn and connect.
🦁
Lion Cub
Activated & protective. Fight, flight or alert freeze — nervous system in overdrive.
🦔
Hedgehog
Collapsed & shutdown. Withdrawn, shut down, disconnected from the world.
