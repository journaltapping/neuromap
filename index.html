Three nervous system states

🦉
Owl
Regulated & connected. Calm, curious, able to learn and connect.
🦁
Lion Cub
Activated & protective. Fight, flight or alert freeze — nervous system in overdrive.
🦔
Hedgehog
Collapsed & shutdown. Withdrawn, shut down, disconnected from the world.

This short quiz (12 questions) will identify which state your child is spending the most time in, and why.

About this app

NeuroMap was built for the Journal Tapping Method by Michelle Walsh. NeuroMap uses the three nervous system states framework (Owl, Lion Cub, Hedgehog) to help parents understand and support their children.

The AI guidance in this app is educational and supportive — it is not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you are concerned about your child, please consult a qualified professional.
